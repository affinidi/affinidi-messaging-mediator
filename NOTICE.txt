## Third-party material in this repository

This repository reproduces no third-party source code. Its dependencies are
taken from crates.io (and, for the developer-only interop checks under
`interop/`, from npm) under their own licences; `deny.toml` lists the
licences the build accepts.

`crates/affinidi-messaging-mediator/trust-tasks/` holds copies of Trust Task
specification bundles published by the Trust Tasks registry
(https://trusttasks.org). `crates/affinidi-messaging-mediator/trust-tasks.lock`
records the registry URL and the SHA-256 digest of each bundle. They are
reproduced unmodified and remain under the terms the registry publishes them
with.
